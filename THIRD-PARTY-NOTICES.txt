FFmpeg and FFprobe binaries are distributed by ffmpeg-static / ffprobe-static.
Their actual build configuration and GPL notices can be inspected with:
  node_modules\ffmpeg-static\ffmpeg.exe -version
  node_modules\ffmpeg-static\ffmpeg.exe -L
  node_modules\ffprobe-static\bin\win32\x64\ffprobe.exe -version
These builds may include GPL components such as libx264. The binary-specific
licenses and source availability obligations remain applicable when sharing
a portable build. Preserve the included license files. Before distributing
the bundled binaries publicly, supply the matching source code / required
source offer for the precise upstream builds under their respective licenses.
